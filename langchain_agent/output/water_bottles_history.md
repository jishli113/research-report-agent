# Water Bottle History and Evolution

## Key Historical Milestones

**Ancient Times:**
- Early humans used animal skins and bladders to store water
- Egyptians and Greeks developed clay vessels around 1st century BCE
- Bronze and copper containers provided greater durability

**Medieval to Early Modern (1600s-1700s):**
- Glass blowing (1st century BCE) revolutionized water storage for its hygienic properties
- Leather bottles used for travel and military purposes
- 1622: First recorded water bottling at Holy Well in UK with commercial bottling by entrepreneurs

**19th-20th Century Development:**
- 1800s: Tin and aluminum canteens became standard for military and outdoor use
- 1950s: Polyethylene (PE) development made plastic bottles lightweight and cheap
- 1970s: PET (polyethylene terephthalate) introduced, becoming standard for single-use bottles
- Late 1900s: Environmental concerns sparked return to metal bottles (stainless steel and aluminum)

## Material Evolution
The progression from animal skins → pottery → glass → metals → plastics → BPA-free alternatives reflects both technological advancement and changing environmental awareness.

## Modern Era (2000s-Present)
- Rise of reusable bottle movement due to plastic pollution awareness
- Development of BPA-free plastics
- Emergence of smart water bottles with hydration tracking
- Market shift toward sustainable, durable alternatives
